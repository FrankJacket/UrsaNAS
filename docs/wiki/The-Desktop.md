# The Desktop

After you sign in, UrsaNAS shows a **desktop**, a bit like the one on your computer: a colourful icon for every feature, over a background you can choose. Click an icon and the feature opens in its own **window**, on top of the desktop.

## The icons

The icons are grouped by purpose:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **System** | Dashboard, Network, Terminal, Appearance, System settings |
| **Storage** | Disks, ZFS pools, Datasets, Remote storage, Backups |
| **Sharing** | Shares, Services, File Manager |
| **Virtualization** | Virtual machines, and Docker (only when Docker is installed and running) |
| **Accounts** | Users, Groups, Administrators |
| **Monitoring** | Alerts, Audit log |

A small red or orange number on the **Alerts** icon tells you how many alerts need your attention.

## Working with windows

- **Open** a feature by clicking its icon. Each feature has one window: clicking its icon again brings the existing window back to the front instead of opening a second one.
- **Move** a window by dragging its title bar, and **resize** it by dragging its edges.
- The buttons in the title bar **minimise** the window (it shrinks to the taskbar), **maximise** it (it fills the screen) or **close** it.
- You can keep several windows open at once and switch between them. They stay open when you go back to the desktop.

## The taskbar

The bar along the bottom of the screen has:

- A **home button** on the left, which takes you back to the desktop.
- A button for **every open window**. Click one to bring its window to the front, or to bring it back if you minimised it. A button that looks outlined is a window that's showing; a plain one is minimised.
- On the right, an **alert indicator** whenever something needs attention. It turns red for critical alerts. Click it to open the **Alerts** window.

## The top bar

The bar along the top is always there, whatever you're doing:

- **Terminal** opens a command line on the NAS, in a window. It's meant for administrators who know what they're doing: everything you type runs with full rights, and the session is recorded in the **Audit log**. Most people never need it.
- Your **username**, and a button to **sign out**.
- The **language** menu: English or French.
- The **theme** menu: light or dark. For more choices, see [Appearance](Appearance).
- The **power button**: **Restart** or **Shut down** the NAS. Use shut down before unplugging the machine or moving it.

## A tip for small screens

On a narrow screen, windows are cramped. Use the **maximise** button so a window fills the screen; the content then has the most room, and long lists scroll inside the window.
