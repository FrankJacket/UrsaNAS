# Network

The **Network** page shows every network connection the NAS has, whether it's currently connected, its address, and a live view of how much data is going in and out.

## Checking a connection

Click a connection to expand it and see its details: IP address, speed, and whether it's set to get its address automatically (DHCP) or has one you've set by hand.

## Changing settings

Click **Edit** on a connection to change its address, or **Settings** at the top of the page for options that apply to the NAS as a whole. Most people never need to change anything here — the NAS works out of the box on a typical home network with automatic addressing.

## Advanced options

Two more advanced features are available for larger or more demanding setups, and are entirely optional:

- **Link Aggregation** combines two or more network cables into one faster, more reliable connection.
- **Open vSwitch** is a more flexible way to share one network card between the NAS and its virtual machines.

If you don't already know you need either of these, you don't — the defaults are fine.
