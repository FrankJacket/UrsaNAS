# Appearance

The **Appearance** window (in the **System** group of the [desktop](The-Desktop)) lets you make UrsaNAS look the way you like. Changes apply immediately, so you see the result as you click.

## Colors

Choose a **color scheme**. Each one changes more than the buttons: it tints the page backgrounds, the panels, the lines between items, the top bar and the title bars of windows, so every scheme has its own look.

- **Default** is the original look, with no tinting at all. Pick it whenever you want to go back to how UrsaNAS looked at first.
- The others — **Ocean**, **Lagoon**, **Forest**, **Ember**, **Rose**, **Violet** and **Graphite** — each give the interface its own colour.

The colour schemes work with both the light and the dark theme. Switch between them with the **theme** menu in the top bar; the schemes adjust their shades so the text always stays readable.

## Wallpaper

The wallpaper is the background of the desktop. You can choose:

- **Theme color**, a soft gradient in the colour you picked above. This is the default.
- One of the **provided wallpapers**: Aurora, Dusk, Midnight, Dune and Mist.
- **My image**, a picture of your own. Click **Upload an image** and pick a PNG, JPEG or WebP file of up to 10 MB. It is used straight away, and **Remove my image** deletes it again.

## What is remembered, and for whom

- Your **colour scheme**, **theme** (light or dark) and **wallpaper choice** are kept in this browser. Another browser or another computer starts from the default again, so set them once on each device you use.
- An **uploaded image** is stored on the NAS and is **shared by everyone who uses it**: if one administrator uploads a picture, it's the same picture for the others who choose "My image".
